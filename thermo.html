THERMO
GUNS

A game weapon concept inspired by thermodynamic processes like isothermal, adiabatic, isochoric, and isobaric systems.

Project Overview

Thermo Guns explores how engineering concepts can become gameplay systems: pressure, temperature, energy, cooldowns, and process cycles translated into weapon behavior.
