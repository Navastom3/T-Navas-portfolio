The Band

What was G-Block?

G-Block was a year-long music class made up of selected top music students from my high school. Throughout the year, we start developping chemistry and getting the ropes with covers, representing our school in competitions and diverse events and all leading up to the writing of our original song, "Paradise" to end up recording it along with our band's pick songs at the end of the year .”

It became one more space where I experienced what it feels like to build something creative with a team over time. The band taught me collaboration, improvisation, stage presence, the importance of showing up regularly and mindfully, and how much better ideas can become when people build on each other’s strengths.

01

Paradise

The final product

Our original song, “Paradise,” written and recorded by the band, was the culmination of our year together. It blends rock, pop, and funk influences with catchy hooks, energetic fills, and dynamic arrangements. Looking back, we agreed that we were most proud of isn't any individual part of the song—it's the fact that it genuinely sounds like us. More than anything, the process showed us how much better an idea can become when everyone contributes to building it and listens to each other.

02

Intraschool competitions

representing menor

Throughout the year, we represented our school in several interschool music competitions. Beyond the competition itself, these events were a great way to build chemistry as a band and perform in front of new audiences. At La Birreria, we reached the finals, earned a paid performance opportunity, and I was awarded Best Keyboardist. Later, at UDLA, several members of the band were selected for musical scholarships in recognition of our performances. Looking back, these competitions were some of the most rewarding milestones of the year.

03

Playing local

School Events

As the school's top band, we performed at major school events and concerts, including the annual Choreography Competition. In front of an audience of more than 1,500 students, families, and alumni, we opened the night with a MIDI mashup spanning over 20 songs that captured the Menor spirit. Our role was to set the energy for the entire event and get the crowd engaged before the main performances began.
